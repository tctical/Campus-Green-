Why small campus actions matter

Each reusable bottle, switched off light, and recycled notebook seems minor on its own, but together these habits create cleaner spaces and stronger communities.

Students can lead local change by choosing refill stations, reducing single-use plastics, sharing unused materials, and supporting low-energy habits in dorms and study rooms. This page is designed to work offline, making it easy to revisit simple actions anywhere on campus.

Simple green actions

Focus on actions that are easy to repeat. Consistency is more powerful than perfection, especially in a busy student routine.

Open the weekly checklist
  • Carry one refillable bottle and mug.
  • Unplug chargers when they are not in use.
  • Walk or share transport for short campus trips.
  • Print only when digital notes are not suitable.
